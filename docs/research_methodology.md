# Research Methodology

## AI-Driven Global Financial Contagion and Geopolitical Risk Prediction Engine

**Author:** Harsh Singh  
**Research Domain:** Quantitative Finance, Financial Contagion,
Geopolitical Risk, Systemic Risk, Artificial Intelligence and Network Science

**Research Status:** Proposed Research Framework

---

## 1. Research Background

Global financial markets are interconnected through
cross-border banking, capital flows, trade relationships,
corporate exposures and financial instruments.

A geopolitical shock originating in one country may
transmit financial stress across multiple countries,
institutions and asset classes.

This research proposes a multimodal AI framework
to investigate these transmission mechanisms.

The proposed Multimodal AI Contagion Engine (MACE)
integrates financial, macroeconomic, geopolitical,
network and textual intelligence.

## 2. Research Problem

Conventional financial risk models may not adequately
represent the interaction between geopolitical events,
dynamic financial networks, nonlinear transmission
and regime-dependent contagion.

The research investigates whether integrating these
information sources can improve systemic-risk
forecasting and early-warning capabilities.

## 3. Primary Research Objectives

### PO1. Multimodal Contagion Prediction

Develop a multimodal framework integrating heterogeneous
financial and geopolitical information for systemic
contagion prediction.

Measurement:
Benchmark Improvement Score and predictive AUC
relative to single-modality baselines.

### PO2. Global Financial Knowledge Graph

Design a dynamic financial knowledge graph representing
cross-border financial and economic relationships.

Measurement:
Graph completeness and data coverage.

### PO3. Network-Aware Prediction

Investigate whether network-aware models improve
contagion-path forecasting compared with flat-feature
and isolated-country models.

Measurement:
Network Prediction Advantage and contagion-path accuracy.

### PO4. Early-Warning Intelligence

Develop a regime-aware early-warning research framework
for detecting emerging systemic financial stress.

Measurement:
Detection advance lead time and precision-recall performance.

### PO5. Geopolitical Transmission Analysis

Investigate how geopolitical events propagate through
financial, trade and institutional exposure networks.

Measurement:
Shock transmission accuracy and network propagation analysis.

## 4. Secondary Research Objectives

1. Construct a point-in-time-safe data architecture.
2. Develop financial and geopolitical feature engineering.
3. Integrate multilingual textual intelligence.
4. Investigate nonlinear contagion mechanisms.
5. Develop causal inference and explainability methods.
6. Design historical crisis replay and stress testing.
7. Establish model governance and reproducibility protocols.

## 5. Research Questions

RQ1. Can multimodal AI identify emerging financial
contagion earlier than conventional indicators?

RQ2. Do financial network representations improve
cross-country contagion prediction?

RQ3. Does multimodal information provide incremental
predictive value over individual data modalities?

RQ4. Do contagion mechanisms differ across normal,
stressed and crisis regimes?

RQ5. Do nonlinear transmission mechanisms explain
extreme financial contagion more effectively than
linear alternatives?

RQ6. Can geopolitical shocks generate multi-stage
cross-border financial cascades?

## 6. Six Formal Research Hypotheses

### H1 — Early-Warning Hypothesis

A multimodal AI system can identify emerging systemic
financial stress earlier than conventional indicators.

Testing approach:
Compare detection lead times, AUC and precision-recall
performance against established baseline indicators.

Proposed research target:
Investigate a 14-day detection lead-time advantage.

### H2 — Network Advantage Hypothesis

Network-aware models outperform isolated-country
or isolated-asset models in forecasting contagion.

Testing approach:
Compare TGNN and other network-aware models against
flat-feature and non-network baselines.

### H3 — Multimodal Intelligence Hypothesis

Combining market, macroeconomic, geopolitical,
textual and network information improves predictive
performance relative to single-modality approaches.

Testing approach:
Perform modality ablation experiments and compare
out-of-sample predictive performance.

### H4 — Regime-Dependency Hypothesis

Contagion mechanisms vary materially across normal,
stressed and crisis regimes.

Testing approach:
Compare regime-specific model parameters using
likelihood-ratio tests and regime-conditioned analysis.

### H5 — Nonlinear Transmission Hypothesis

Financial contagion exhibits nonlinear transmission,
threshold effects and amplification that linear
models cannot adequately represent.

Testing approach:
Compare linear and nonlinear models using
tail-event performance, AUC and specification tests.

### H6 — Cascade Dynamics Hypothesis

An initial geopolitical shock can produce multi-stage
cross-border financial cascades through interconnected
networks.

Testing approach:
Use historical crisis replay, network propagation
analysis and cascade simulations.

## 7. Research Design

Research type:
Quantitative, computational, predictive and
network-based research.

Research approach:
A combination of financial econometrics,
machine learning, deep learning, graph learning,
NLP and causal inference.

Research unit:
Country, financial institution, financial asset,
geopolitical event and network relationship.

Research dimensions:

- Temporal dimension
- Cross-country dimension
- Financial network dimension
- Geopolitical event dimension
- Multimodal information dimension

## 8. Proposed Methodological Framework

### Stage 1 — Data Collection

Collect authorised and documented data covering:

- Global financial markets
- Macroeconomic indicators
- Sovereign and credit risk
- Geopolitical events
- Banking and corporate exposures
- Trade and supply chains
- News and official communications
- Alternative data

### Stage 2 — Data Processing

Perform:

- Data validation
- Missing-value analysis
- Outlier treatment
- Currency harmonisation
- Temporal alignment
- Point-in-time verification
- Data provenance tracking

### Stage 3 — Feature Engineering

Construct financial, macroeconomic,
geopolitical, textual and network features.

### Stage 4 — Network Construction

Represent countries, institutions, assets,
corporations and geopolitical actors as
nodes and relationships as edges.

### Stage 5 — Baseline Modelling

Establish econometric and statistical benchmarks:

- Logistic Regression
- DCC-GARCH
- Panel VAR
- Random Forest
- XGBoost

### Stage 6 — Advanced AI Modelling

Investigate:

- LSTM
- Temporal Convolutional Networks
- Transformers
- Graph Neural Networks
- Graph Attention Networks
- Temporal Graph Neural Networks

### Stage 7 — Multimodal Fusion

Integrate heterogeneous representations
using a proposed cross-modal fusion architecture.

### Stage 8 — Risk Estimation

Investigate:

- Contagion probability
- Systemic-risk indicators
- Risk severity
- Network propagation
- Early-warning signals

### Stage 9 — Validation

Apply chronological walk-forward testing,
cross-country testing, historical crisis replay
and robustness analysis.

### Stage 10 — Interpretation

Evaluate feature importance, network pathways,
uncertainty and counterfactual explanations.

## 9. Research Integrity

The proposed architecture must be distinguished
from experimentally implemented components.

No predictive performance, statistical significance
or hypothesis confirmation should be reported
without reproducible empirical evidence.

Synthetic datasets must be identified explicitly.

All external datasets must have documented
licensing, publication dates and data provenance.

## 10. Limitations

- Rare crisis events create small-sample problems.
- Bilateral exposure data may have reporting delays.
- Proxy variables may introduce measurement error.
- Financial relationships are non-stationary.
- Geopolitical event annotation may be inconsistent.
- Historical performance may not generalise to future crises.

## 11. Expected Scientific Contribution

The proposed research investigates an integrated
framework connecting geopolitical intelligence,
financial network structure, temporal learning,
systemic risk and early-warning analysis.

## 12. Research Status

Status: Proposed research programme.

Empirical confirmation of H1-H6 requires
completed experiments and statistical testing.
